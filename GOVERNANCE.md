# Maintenance and contribution policy

This independent Picco fork is maintained by
[DumbledoreCalrissian](https://github.com/DumbledoreCalrissian).
The fork owner decides its scope, accepts behavior and maintenance obligations,
and approves changes to this repository. CrossPoint and FreeInk maintainers are
responsible for their own projects, not this custom build.

## Proposals and reports

Read [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) before proposing work.
Use this repository's available issue and review channels for downstream changes.
Include the affected revision, selected build profile, reproduction steps and
whether results come from the simulator or physical hardware. Investigate an
upstream report against an appropriate upstream revision before directing it there.

An accepted downstream feature need not fit upstream's product policy. Submission
upstream is a separate decision and follows upstream's own contribution rules.
Keep changes focused and document their resource, storage and maintenance costs.
Hardware checks and publication require the owner's explicit authorization.
Agent work follows [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), including its existing commit, push,
review and firmware-handoff requirements.

## Working together

Discuss code and behavior respectfully. Personal attacks and harassment are not
welcome. Use plain English, distinguish evidence from assumptions, and label
planned, implemented, tested, committed and hardware-verified work separately.
Maintaining this fork does not imply affiliation with or endorsement by the
original projects or device manufacturers.

## Provenance

Preserve copyright and licence notices, third-party credits and source history.
[ATTRIBUTIONS.md](ATTRIBUTIONS.md) explains the source foundation and downstream
changes. This repository's product and funding choices are its owner's decisions.
