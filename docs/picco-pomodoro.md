# Pomodoro

Open **Pomodoro** on Home (in the Desk theme, from the menu button on Home). The screen shows the phase, remaining time and
completed focus sessions.

- Defaults: 25-minute focus, 5-minute short break and 15-minute long break
  after four focus sessions.
- Use **Start**, **Pause** and **Resume**. **Reset phase** stops an unfinished
  phase and restores its full length, keeping completed sessions.
- Completion waits at `00:00` until you choose **Start next phase**. There is
  no sound, vibration or automatic next phase.
- Opening Timer settings or Control Center pauses the timer. Choose **Resume**
  to continue afterward.
- Leaving the timer or manually sleeping ends the cycle. Reopening starts fresh;
  it does not run in the background or send reminders.
- A running timer prevents auto-sleep. Ready, paused and completed timers use
  the normal sleep timeout.

The countdown normally repaints about every ten seconds, so it can display an
older value between updates.

## Settings

Tap the header gear. Tap a value to adjust it; Back or a tap outside cancels.

| Setting | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focus | 5–90 minutes | 25 minutes |
| Short break | 1–30 minutes | 5 minutes |
| Long break | 5–60 minutes | 15 minutes |
| Focus sessions per cycle | 2–8 | 4 |

**Reset to defaults** asks for confirmation. Settings persist between launches.
A changed duration applies immediately to a Ready timer. A paused phase keeps
its current duration until Reset or the next phase.

Up/Down selects controls and Confirm activates them. Setting
**Controls → Short click** to **Confirm** lets a short power press activate the
selected control. Touch remains the main way to use the app on Picco.
